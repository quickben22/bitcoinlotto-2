# Dormancy and public attribution for the 19 chains

Dormancy = time from the last transaction that moved at least 0.001 BTC before 12 Nov 2026 (dust deposits excluded) to the first transaction in the 12–19 Nov 2026 window. Several addresses were reused as relay hops inside that window, so measuring from the transaction immediately before each tracked move would understate dormancy. Block times are from the public WalletExplorer and Esplora APIs.

Attribution is from the public WalletExplorer wallet-clustering API. An "unnamed cluster" means the addresses are grouped together on-chain but the group has no public name; a named label (exchange, service, etc.) is a public attribution, not proof of legal ownership.
